01 — Profile

Rebuilding the
Future of Flight

Apexara Dynamics (operating as AGMM Antiquities Inc.) sources disassembled South Korean–made UAV systems through vetted partner companies, assembles the airframes and avionics, and develops modular autonomy software integrable across a full spectrum of combat and defense platforms.

Hardware refurbishment and software development are managed through certified subcontractors and partner firms, delivering finished platforms that meet the exacting requirements of U.S. military and government customers.
