About our Company
What Our Company Does/ Who We Are:


AGMM Antiquities Inc. (S‑Corp) rebuilds drones using surplus components and parts sourced through partner companies abroad. We receive disassembled South Korean‑made systems, assemble the airframes and avionics, and develop modular autonomy software that can be integrated into a range of combat and defense UAVs. Hardware refurbishment and software development are managed through vetted subcontractors and partner firms to meet customer and military requirements.

-Mission Computer (ROS2 [robot operating system] Host): open-source middle-ware. Connects sensors, flight controllers and intelligent algorithms to enable autonomous behavior. Acts as a standardized language for drone software. 


-Power distribution, battery and fuel-cell subsystem: PDM (power distribution module) for fuel-cell controllers H2 platforms with battery management and current sensing.Companies such as Pixhawk for PDM, Doosan for fuel-cell integration. Eaton/ Mean Well for higher power. 
-ESCs, motor controllers and motors: ESC’s (electronic speed controller) control the BLDC (brushless direct current) motor. Companies such as T-Motor, KDE, and Hobbywing. 


-IMU, INS, GNNS: needs compatibility with anti-spoofing. Companies VectorNav (VN-300/400), OxTS, u-blox (F9 series for RTK), Septentrio (defense grade GNNS). 


-EO/IR gimbal and sensors: Gimbal connects EO to IR interface, stabilizes cameras. Companies such as Hanwha (EO modules), FLIR/ Teledyne, WESCAM. 


-Radar/ LiDAR: Compact FMCW Radar, small LiDAR for sense/ and avoiding ISR. Companies Navtech, Echodyne (radar), Velodyne/ RoboSense (LiDAR) 


-Tactical datalinks, radios, SATCOMs: Companies L3Harris, Persistent Systems (drone class modems), Cobham/ Saccade (SATCOM). Modem for SATCOM. 


-EW, Spectrum Sensors (non offensive receiver/ detection): RF spectrum sensor if needed with module jammer units. Companies Rohde & Schwarz (sensors), L3Harris (EW modules), Rhode class offers detection. (jammer units require legal/ operational clearance). 


-Health, black-box, TPM: Encrypted flight data recorder, TPM (Trusted Platform Modulator) /HSM for keys, telemetry logger SSD (solid-state drive). Companies SEAKR (recorders), Infineon (TPM), 


-Mechanical, mounting, connectors, vibration isolation: Elastomeric mounts, EMI shielding, Keyed aviation connectors, harness assemblies. Companies Lord Corp (vibration isolators), Amphenol (aviation connectors), TE Connectivity. Reduces vibration and maintains EMI/ EMC integrity.

Typical tactical quadcopters provide short-range ISR (hover/stare) with excellent agility and
low-altitude sensing, but endurance is limited compared with fixed-wing platforms.
